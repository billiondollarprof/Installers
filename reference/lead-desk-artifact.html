Connecting to your leads…

Paste a list of leads

One lead per line, columns separated by commas or tabs, in this order: business, contact name, trade, town, phone, email, website, company type (Ltd or Sole trader), company number, source, notes. Copying straight from a spreadsheet works. A header row is skipped.

The daily loop

  1. FindIndependent installers with 5 to 30 staff in one region. Google Maps, their own websites, the FENSA installer list and Companies House. Add them here, or send the list to Claude to load in.
  2. Check before you contactIs it a limited company? Companies House answers in seconds, and you can email a limited company without asking first, as long as every email has an opt out. Sole traders and ordinary partnerships need consent before you email them, so phone them instead. Before calling any number, check it is not on the CTPS list (the business opt out register for sales calls).
  3. Call 10 a dayUse the Script tab. Ask the one question, listen, and offer the two minute video only to owners who see the problem.
  4. Send the video the same dayPersonalise the demo with their business name, record it, send it on WhatsApp or email, and set the lead to Video sent. The desk sets the follow up for two days later.
  5. Follow up on the day you promisedOpen Due today each morning. Call, ask what they thought, and offer the pilot.
  6. Close with a deposit£1,500 to set up, £500 to start and the rest when it goes live. Monthly care at £199 is offered after it is live, never before.

What counts

After two weeks, look at the numbers at the top of Leads: how many calls it takes to get one interested owner, and how many interested owners it takes to get one deposit. Change the opening line or the region if the first number is poor; change the offer if the second is.
