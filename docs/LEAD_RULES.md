# What makes a qualified lead

A business is added to the desk only if it passes every rule below. Anything
that fails is skipped and the reason is logged in the run record.

## Must pass

1. **Trade.** Installs one or more of: windows, doors (composite, bifold,
   French, patio), conservatories, orangeries, roofline (fascias, soffits,
   guttering), glass roofs or skylights, secondary glazing.
2. **Sells to homeowners.** Exclude trade only fabricators, manufacturers and
   suppliers (look for "trade only", "trade counter", "fabricator" on the site).
3. **Active limited company** on Companies House (company status "active").
   Sole traders and partnerships are kept in a separate "phone only" list,
   never emailed (see Legal in DECISIONS.md).
4. **Trading three years or more** (incorporation date), so they have a backlog
   of old quotes.
5. **Independent, roughly 5 to 30 staff.** Signals: 20 to 400 Google reviews,
   one to three branches. Exclude national brands and franchises (for example
   Anglian, Everest, Safestyle, Zenith) and anything with many locations.
6. **Has a website with a quote or enquiry form**, so they actually issue quotes.
7. **Reachable:** a phone number from Places and an email from their own site.
8. **Inside the chosen region** (registered office or trading address).

## Useful Companies House SIC codes (check against the live list before use)

- 43342 Glazing
- 43320 Joinery installation
- 43390 Other building completion and finishing
- 43910 Roofing activities (roofline firms)

Many installers file under a general code, so also match by business name
words: windows, glazing, doors, conservatories, roofline, fascias.

## Score (0 to 100), highest first on the desk

- Google reviews: more reviews, more quote volume (cap the benefit at 400).
- Rating 4.3 or higher.
- Recent reviews in the last 90 days: active business.
- Company age 3 to 20 years.
- Director name found (the call opens with a name).
- Generic sales email found (info@, sales@, enquiries@).

## Per lead, store

Business name, director or contact name, trades, town, region, phone, email,
website, company type, company number, incorporation date, review count,
rating, score, Places place id, source, CTPS status (unchecked until the owner
checks), pipeline status, next follow up date, last contact, notes.
