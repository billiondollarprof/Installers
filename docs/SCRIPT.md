# Call script

The live, fill in the blanks version is in the Lead Desk's Script tab. Keep
the two in step: change one, change the other.

## Before you dial
Have their website open. Know the director's name if Companies House lists
one. Aim for two to three minutes.

## 1. Opener
"Hi, is that {name}? My name is {me}. I'll be quick. I help window and door
companies win back quotes that went quiet. Can I ask you one question?"

If someone else answers: "Who looks after quotes and sales there? When is the
best time to catch them?"

## 2. The question
"When you send a quote and the customer stops replying, what usually happens
to it?"
Then stop talking and listen.

## 3. Dig a little
"Roughly how many quotes do you send in a month?"
"How many of those would you say you chase more than once?"
"What about the ones from six months ago, does anyone go back to those?"

## 4. Bridge
"That's what most owners tell me. Those quotes aren't lost, they just went
quiet. I've built a system that messages them politely in your name, asks if
they're still interested, and books the ones who are into a call with you.
Then it does the same for every new quote, so none go quiet again."

## 5. The ask
"Can I send you a two minute video showing it working with {biz} on the
screen? Is WhatsApp or email better for you?"
"Great. I'll send it today. Can I give you a ring on {day} to hear what you
think? Morning or afternoon?"

## If they say
- **"We already follow up."** Good, that puts you ahead of most. How about the
  quotes from six months or a year ago? That's usually where the money is.
- **"Not interested."** No problem at all, thanks for your time. Mark it, move
  on, never push.
- **"How much?"** It's a one off setup of £1,500: £500 to start and the rest
  when it's live and working. The video shows exactly what you'd get.
- **"Is this spam?"** It only messages people who asked you for a quote, in
  your name, with an opt out on every message. No reply after two messages and
  it stops.
- **"Is it AI?"** The first messages are set by you, word for word. If a
  customer types a question, an assistant answers from your own information,
  and anything it can't answer goes straight to you.
- **"Email me something."** Of course, what's the best address? I'll send the
  video and call {day}.
- **"Where are you based?"** I work remotely with UK businesses.

## Follow up call, two days later
"Hi {name}, it's {me}. I sent you the video on {sentday}. Did you get a chance
to watch it? What did you think? The simplest way to try it is on your old
quotes. You send me the quotes from the last six to twelve months, I set it up
in your name, and you see the replies come in. It's £500 to start and the rest
when it's live. Shall we get you set up?"

you. {me}

## The two minute video
1. Their name and the problem (15 s): "Hi {name}, this is for {biz}. Every
   quote that goes quiet is a customer who already asked you for a price."
2. The demo (60 s): their name in it, tap through Sarah booking and the alert.
3. The money (20 s): the "What it's worth" screen.
4. The ask (15 s): "I'll call you {day} to hear what you think."
